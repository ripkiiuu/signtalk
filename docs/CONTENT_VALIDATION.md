## Recognition and assessment

MediaPipe Hand Landmarker produces hand landmarks only. SIGNTALK has no trained ASL class recognizer, labelled dataset, subject-independent validation, or component-level evaluator. Recognition status is `model_unavailable`; no classifier score or ASL correctness claim is emitted.
